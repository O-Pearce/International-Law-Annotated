What is International Law Annotated?

We’re building a global, open-access layer of commentary on key international legal texts. Each page embeds concise, scholarly context beneath each paragraph number.

The goal is clarity and cross-referencing: to make international law more navigable for students, practitioners, and researchers alike.

Readers should, however, exercise care - annotations have not been thoroughly peer-reviewed.
